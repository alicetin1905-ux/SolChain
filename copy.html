Simulation rules

  • No real money. Nothing is bought or sold. This page shows what copying would have done.
  • Buys. When a followed wallet buys a token against SOL, USDC or USDT, each strategy buys 1 SOL of it, once per wallet and token.
  • Skipped buys. A buy is skipped when the token has less than $25k liquidity, when its creator can still mint new tokens or freeze holders' tokens, when its 10 largest holders (the pool left out) own more than 40%, or when the live price is already more than 15% above the wallet's own.
  • Exits. Every strategy sells the same share as the wallet whenever it sells. On top of that, "−30%" sells everything once the price is 30% below entry; "2× then trail" sells half at double the entry and the rest once the price falls 30% below its peak; "24 hours" sells everything a day after entry. These exits are checked every minute on the live engine and every hour otherwise.
  • Prices. On the live engine, copies of the wallet's trades are priced with a real Jupiter quote at the moment it reacts. Otherwise, and for the strategy exits, a fill is 3% worse than the price used. Every fill pays 0.001 SOL in fees. Partial sells worth under 0.02 SOL are skipped.
Data: Solana RPC, Jupiter, Dexscreener public API. Not financial advice.
