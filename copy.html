Simulation rules

  • No real money. Nothing is bought or sold. This page shows what copying would have done.
  • Buys. When a followed wallet buys a token against SOL, USDC or USDT, the engine buys 1 SOL of it, once per wallet and token. Tokens with less than $25k liquidity are skipped.
  • Sells. When that wallet sells, the engine sells the same share of its copy. Selling everything closes the position.
  • Costs. Each fill is 3% worse than the wallet's own price, for arriving after it, plus 0.001 SOL in fees.
  • Timing. A GitHub Action checks the wallets every hour. A live engine would react within seconds, but the simulated prices are based on the wallet's own fill, so the hourly check mainly delays when trades show up here.
Data: Solana RPC, Dexscreener public API. Not financial advice.
