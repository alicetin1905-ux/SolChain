How this is worked out

  • Tokens. Every pump.fun token starts on its own bonding curve before it moves to a regular pool. The first trades on that curve are the launch.
  • First buyers. For each token, the first 60 wallets that bought on the curve are recorded, in order, with their entry price. The token's creator is left out.
  • Snipers. A buy in the launch block or the next one is flagged as a sniper or a bundle, often the creator's own wallets or bots. A wallet that sniped most of its tokens is hidden by default and never copied.
  • Copying. The 5 best repeat early buyers that are not snipers are added to the copy-trade engine, next to the leaderboard's top 10.
  • Limits. Tokens launched elsewhere than pump.fun, and tokens with more than 20,000 trades on their curve, are not analysed. Being early does not mean the wallet sold well; the copy-trade page shows what following it is worth.
